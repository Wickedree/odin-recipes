Chocolate Chips Cookies

Chocolate chip cookies are warm, soft, and packed with delicious chocolate chips in every bite. With a golden, slightly crispy edge and a soft, chewy center, they’re the perfect sweet treat. The melted chocolate adds a rich, gooey flavor that makes them impossible to resist.

INGREDIENTS:

  • butter, softened
  • white sugar
  • brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • Vanilla extract
  • Baking Soda
  • Hot water
  • Satl
  • All-purpose flour
  • Semisweet chocolate chips
  • Chopped walnuts

STEPS:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Beat butter, white sugar, and brown sugar together in a large bowl with an electric mixer until smooth and creamy.
  2. Beat in eggs, one at a time, then stir in vanilla.
  3. Dissolve baking soda in hot water; add to batter along with salt and mix until combined.
  4. Stir in flour, chocolate chips, and walnuts until a soft dough forms.
  5. Drop rounded spoonfuls of cookie dough 2 inches apart onto ungreased baking sheets.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven until edges are lightly browned, about 10 minutes.
  7. Cool on the baking sheets briefly before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.
  8. Store in an airtight container or serve immediately and enjoy!
