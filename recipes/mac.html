Mac and Cheese

Home

Description

Creamy, cheesy, and delicious, this mac and cheese is the ultimate comfort food. Tender macaroni is coated in a rich, velvety cheese sauce and baked until warm, bubbly, and golden on top. Every bite is gooey, flavorful, and satisfying.

INGREDIENTS:

  • 1 (8 ounce) box elbow macaroni
  • butter
  • all-purpose flour
  • salt
  • ground black pepper to taste
  • milk
  • shredded Cheddar cheese

STEPS:

  1. Cook elbow macaroni in boiling water, stirring occasionally until tender yet firm to the bite, about 8 minutes.
  2. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat.
  3. Whisk in flour, salt, and black pepper until smooth, about 5 minutes.
  4. Slowly pour in milk, while constantly stirring; cook and stir until mixture is smooth and bubbling, about 5 minutes, making sure the milk doesn’t burn.
  5. Add Cheddar cheese and stir until melted, 2 to 4 minutes.
  6. Drain macaroni and fold into cheese sauce until coated.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy!
